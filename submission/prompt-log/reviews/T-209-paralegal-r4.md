**Does it contradict the note above it?** I checked each reason (walk JSON, 1280x900 texts):
- `two_digit_year`: no clash. The note says to confirm the date from the highlighted words, and the sentence agrees.
- `ambiguous_numeric`, `several_dates`, `no_year`, `invalid`, `implausible`: the two lines give different first steps. The note ends "Confirm the date from the highlighted words and fill the blank". The sentence says "find the date of service in the record (the highlighted words may help) or ask the provider". The sentence does not say the opposite of the note. It is wider than the note, and it is the safe route: neither line tells her to copy the highlighted date anymore. The harmful part is in the note itself. For these five reasons, the note's first sentence says the highlighted words cannot settle the date or that the date is wrong, and its last sentence says to confirm the date from those same words. That wording is binding decision text (`memory/ORCHESTRATOR_DECISIONS.md:88` "asking the user to confirm the date from the quote"; `:93` "The paralegal fills it from the quote"). It is not in the 1b3c58a diff, and changing it needs a decision. One wording clash is new: for `ambiguous_numeric`, the note says "the record does not settle which" and the sentence says "find the date of service in the record". **Severity: minor.** The letter prints the blank whatever she does. The note names the defect (Feb 30, after the statement, two readings), and the sentence now gives her a way out to the provider. In round 3, the sentence itself pointed her at the wrong date. That was the major, and it is gone.
- `no_date` and `non_service_date`: the note says "Request a dated line-by-line bill, or fill the blank from the record". The sentence agrees, except for "(the highlighted words may help)". On a `no_date` line, the note says "The line states no date of service." On my DOB variant, the only date in the highlighted words is a birth date. So round 3's "never points at the highlighted words" is partly undone, in a hedged form. Minor.

**Are the amount and date line visible?** Yes, at both brief viewports, for every reason and the control. One reason still clips the sentence: `non_service_date` (the longest note) at 1366x768. The foot is clientHeight 184 against scrollHeight 200, so "...adds the amount to the bill total." is cut. The e2e layout test does not include that reason.
