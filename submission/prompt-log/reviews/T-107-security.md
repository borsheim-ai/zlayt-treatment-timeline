## Three new OCR-path attacks (all against `ocr=true`, one server per group, `probe_attacks.py`)

All 31 files finished as a 400 or a 201, server RSS never left its idle figure on the 25 dpi and image files, and no child or tesseract process was left 3 s after any of them.

1. **Many pages** (`build_attacks.py`, `build_speckle.py`): 11 and 100 image-only pages: `400 ocr_too_many_pages` in 0.2 s. 50000 `Kids` entries pointing at one page (294 KB): `413 pdf_too_large` in 0.3 s. A 3000-deep page tree (290 KB): `400 pdf_unreadable` in 0.1 s. Ten 2880 pt pages of noise and of speckle: `400 ocr_images_too_large` in 0.8 s and 2.1 s (the 12 MB PNG cap is enforced in the child). Ten pages that do fit (a 92 KB and a 147 KB file): `201`, 80.2 s and 86.8 s, health max 264 ms, server RSS 59 to 166 to 222 MB retained across the two cases (stored page images; see questions). One speckle page that Tesseract struggles with (a 44 KB file, 4000 specks): `400 ocr_failed` at 40.3 s, the per-page timeout. So one small upload can hold a core for up to about 40 s per page and 150 s per request; MAX_PDF_JOBS = 2 caps the concurrency. It is bounded, no rate limit exists by D-020, and I am not filing it.
2. **Huge DPI through parameters**: the route takes only `name`, `ocr` and `include_hidden` (`api.py:251`); there is no dpi, scale or language parameter and unknown query parameters are ignored. The size can only come from the file, so I carried it there (11 files): MediaBox 1e9 and -1e9..1e9: `400 ocr_page_too_large` in 0.2 to 0.3 s; 2880 pt page: rendered, 0.6 s, child peak 53 MB; UserUnit 14400 on a 100 pt page, CropBox 100 on a 14400 MediaBox, CropBox 99999999 on a 100 pt page, 0.001 pt and zero MediaBox, 2880 by 0.01 pt, Rotate 90 on 2880 by 20: all `400 pdf_no_text_layer` in 0.3 to 0.4 s with child peak 18 to 53 MB. A 2 KB file with nested Form XObjects (9 levels of 10 draws, 1e9 draws): the child hit 1031 MB, was killed by the RSS watch, `400 ocr_check_failed` at 1.9 s, server RSS 57 MB the whole time.
3. **Malformed image streams** (14 files): declared 100000 by 100000 and 2e9 by 2e9 and negative sizes over short Flate data, a DCT stream with garbage and one with a 65535 by 65535 header, JPX and JBIG2 garbage, CCITT at 1000000 columns, a Predictor 15 with Columns 2e9, LZW garbage, a 21-stage filter chain, an Indexed palette short of data, a self-referencing SMask, and an inline image declared 60000 by 60000: every one `400 pdf_no_text_layer` in 0.3 to 0.9 s (OCR ran on a blank render), child peak 18 to 95 MB, server RSS 57 MB, health max 11 ms. PDFium refused or skipped each one instead of allocating; none reached the crash path.
