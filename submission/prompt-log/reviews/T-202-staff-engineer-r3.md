# T-202 (D-071 amendment, addendum) staff-engineer review, round 3, commit 973b542

Verdict: **changes** (0 blockers, 1 major, 2 minors, 1 nit).

Every round 2 finding is fixed and I re-verified each from a `git archive 973b542` copy. The resolver (`app/backend/service_date.py`) is now an allow-list for bills: a date prints only with a service label in its clause (DOS, date of service, service date, seen on, visit date, run of), a table heading that names service over the row's date cell, or the one period the bill states for a coded line. My round 2 leaks all print the fillable blank now: 30 single-column headings (only `DOS` and `Service Date` stay stated; `Date` alone, `Entry date`, `Transaction Date`, `Issued`, `Printed`, `Recorded`, `Filed`, `Remit Date` and the rest give the blank), `Date of injury`, `DOB`, `Authorization`, `Pre-authorized`, `DOI`, `accident`, `referred`, `claim filed`, a service date after its statement, 1998 and 2031. Sentence forms `Entry date:`, `Posted date`, `Transaction date`, `Issue date` give the blank. Labeled dates still print (`DOS 03/13/2026` after a date of injury, `Seen on`, `Service dates ... to ...`, table `Date of Service`, `Date` plus `Date of Service`, `Posted` plus `Service Date`). `all` to `any` is killed (M1). Unit: 6127 passed, 6 skipped. Guided e2e: 143 passed. `python -m evals.letter_check`: value 0 over 25 cases, and two runs of the 84-line charge-header dump are byte-identical.

`evals/letter_check.charge_date_violations` still has its own scanner (no import of `service_date`, `_DOS_RX` or `_dos_iso`) and now works from an allow-list: of 33 header probes it flags the wrong date, a date printed for an unlabeled, DOI, DOB, authorization or Entry/Transaction/Issued/Printed/bare-Date column quote, the old `service date not stated` form, a blank with the wrong statement date, a blank with nine underscores, the bare statement date, a blank above a certain labeled date and a dropped period end; it passes the correct date, the correct blank and a `DOS` column.

The two worker edits outside their paths (one assertion each, `tests/e2e/guided/test_round2.py:333` and `tests/e2e/guided/test_t205.py:91`) swap the old text for the new blank text. The first is still an `in` check of the full blank string plus `Date of service`; the second is still a `re.fullmatch` of the blank with or without a statement date, and the next lines still compare the page text to `sd["text"]` and the letter. Neither weakens anything. The `test_t200` and `test_t202_charge` text swaps in unit are mechanical too (one fixture gains `DOS` so the line stays dated).

What is still wrong: the allow-list stops at bills. A record that the closed-list bill detector does not call a bill takes the record's header date as the date of service with no evidence on the line, and `letter_check` shares that default, so it cannot see it.
