That pass mostly measures memorization. 115 of the 140 round-1 probe sentences are now verbatim lexicon `examples`. On **40 new probes** (clinical and adjuster-style, none copied from the examples) the lexicon scores **11/40**:
- 23 of 31 should-fire probes are FN.
- 6 of 9 should-stay-quiet probes are FP.

Root causes are concrete regex gaps:
- The `(?:\w+\s+)` slots can't match a spinal level ("Prior C5-6 fusion" gets no hit).
- There is no "since <year>" branch.
- The hedge set has no bare "may be <finding>".
- `patient_attributes_causation` has no "to" connector, so "attributes/relates … to the collision" never matches.
- High-strength entries fire on post-accident follow-up wording.

None of these produced a wrong summary I could observe. There is no pipeline output to observe yet. Each one, though, disables a code guard (R-TL-8, R-TL-10(b), source_kind) that exists to stop a §5 dealbreaker. Per my calibration (a guard that can't catch something, with no wrong output yet, is major), they are majors.
