Round-1 majors, one by one:
1. Counts refresh after decisions: PARTLY FIXED, still a major. The "n of N decided" bars, the "Can export, N not reviewed" count and the filters refresh, and a case with every item decided turns green "Everything decided" and leaves Needs attention. But the amber "N need review" chip stays when the paralegal decides the needs-review items first (the order the list sorts them in) while other items are still open. Truck rear-end: I dismissed its four needs-review items in the UI, the detail chip vanished and the header said "7 of 14 decided", and after "All cases" the row still said "4 need review" beside "7 of 14 decided". Cause: the server never sends hitl.needs_review_open (git grep finds it only in overview.js), so overview.js:64 falls back to min(needs_review, undecided), which is only right when almost nothing else is open.
2. Loading and error states: FIXED. "Loading cases..." appears after 250 ms, a list 500 or network failure shows a role=alert sentence and a Try again button that works, an open 404 stays visible for the whole 3 s I sampled (59 of 60 samples), a failed analysis row names the next step. Leftovers are minor (below).
3. One merged block with the first entry on the first screen: FIXED at 1440 (block 229-286 px, first entry and its Accept at y 620-655 of 900; Case checks appears once, "Open decisions" text is gone). Residual at 390 is a minor.
4. Amber "Can export, N not reviewed", never green: FIXED on the overview row and the block chip (computed colour rgb(107,68,0) on rgb(255,241,204); green rgb(19,97,58) only on "Everything decided"; the Ready to send filter lists only fully decided cases). NOT fixed on one surface: the expanded Case checks panel inside the block shows a GREEN chip "Ready to export (18 not reviewed)" (data-testid case-verdict, class zs-verdict zs-v-ready). That is a major.

New items: names on create and Rename pass (Enter saves, Escape keeps the old name, empty name says "Type a name for the case.", focus returns to Rename; a nameless paste is titled from its first record header, "Austin EMS run report, Jan 9, 2026"). "Do this first" shows the server's sentence and "Go to it" lands on the named flag in view (focus on the flag article, 360-636 px of 900); after dismissing it the line moved to the next item and the chip count fell 13 to 12. j/k, arrows, Home and End move between rows, j from the page body starts at the first row, typing j or k in the search box does nothing to focus, Enter opens and focus lands on the case heading.

Scores (1-5): design quality 4, originality 3, craft 3, functionality 3. None is 2 or below.

Re-score against competitors (public product knowledge only; I did not open EvenUp, Supio, Filevine or Litify): the overview now has the things round 1 said were missing against Filevine and Litify lists, namely title search, one status pill per row and one "n of N decided" figure. It is still without sortable column headers, owner, deadline or saved views, and at 8 cases a row is 91-169 px at 1440 (6 rows fully on screen), 239-313 px at 390 (1 row). Against EvenUp and Supio (a single matter status such as in progress or ready for review) it is stronger on what is left for the human and on saying what stays out of the letter. The two defects above (stale chip, green panel chip) are the places where it still tells the paralegal something its own numbers contradict.
